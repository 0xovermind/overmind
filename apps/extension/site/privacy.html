Overmind extension: privacy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

The Overmind browser extension shows the Overmind hive score of a token on its Pons and DexScreener page. This page explains, in full, what it does with data.

What it reads

  • Only the address in the URL of the tab you are viewing, and only on ponsfamily.com and dexscreener.com/robinhood.
  • It does not read the page's content, your wallet, your cookies, your browsing history or anything you type.

What it sends, and where

  • The token (or DexScreener pair) address, to api.overmind.ink to get its hive score, and to api.dexscreener.com to find which token a pair belongs to.
  • Nothing else. No identifiers, no analytics, no tracking, no ads.

What it stores

  • Only where you dragged the card and whether you minimised it, in your browser's local storage on that site. It never leaves your device.

Selling or sharing data

We do not collect personal data, so there is nothing to sell or share. Data is not used for any purpose other than showing the score.

Not financial advice

The score is public on-chain data about what other wallets did. It is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
